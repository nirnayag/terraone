What we do

Innovating for a sustainable future

TerraOne is a modern biotechnology company. Our portfolio combines advanced biology with clean technology to deliver precision nutrition, intelligent release systems, and environmentally elegant performance.

Conventional plastics have transformed industries but at an environmental cost. TerraOne is building the next generation of sustainable biomaterials that perform without leaving a permanent footprint.
